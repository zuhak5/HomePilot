HomePilot Account & Data Deletion

Request permanent deletion of your HomePilot cloud account and associated synchronized data.

What will be deleted?

  • Cloud Database Records: Your synchronized home structures, tasks, rooms, assets, and schedule configurations.
  • Private Media Files: Photos and documents uploaded to your private cloud storage bucket.
  • Account Binding: Your Google authentication association and user identity records.

Data Retention Disclosures

Server-verified AdMob reward transaction logs are retained for fraud prevention, audit compliance, and accounting consistency as required by law and platform agreements.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account using either method below:

  1. In-App Deletion: Open HomePilot → Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  2. Web Deletion (This Page): Authenticate with Google below to verify ownership and confirm deletion.
