HomePilot Account & Data Deletion

Permanently delete your HomePilot cloud account and associated synchronized data without reinstalling or opening the Android app.

What will be deleted?

  • Cloud database records: synchronized home structures, tasks, rooms, assets, schedules, and account-scoped application data.
  • Private media files: photos and documents stored under your private HomePilot user prefix.
  • Authentication account: your HomePilot Supabase authentication user and active sessions.

Data retention

Security, fraud-prevention, server-verified AdMob reward transaction, and legally required audit records may be retained when HomePilot has a legitimate obligation to do so. Retained records are not used to recreate your deleted account.

This browser flow cannot erase files that exist only on a separately installed Android device. Removing the cloud account invalidates account-scoped synchronization; uninstalling or clearing app storage controls device-local files.

How deletion works

  1. In app: HomePilot → Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  2. On the web: sign in with the Google account bound to HomePilot, review the verified identity, then explicitly confirm permanent deletion below.

HomePilot never asks you to type a user ID or email address as authorization. The deletion backend derives identity from the authenticated Supabase session.
